Files shows what is on your phone's storage and helps you keep it tidy.

• See how much space you use, split into photos, videos, audio, documents, apps and other files
• Browse every folder, SD card and USB drive as a list or a grid, and folders other apps share
• Select several files at once to share, delete, copy, move, tag or compress them
• View photos, PDFs and text, and play videos and music, right in the app
• Compress files to ZIP and extract archives, with a look inside first
• Clean up: large files, duplicates, old screenshots, old downloads, empty folders and unused apps
• Search by name with filters for type, date and size, or inside one folder
• Keep private files in a Safe folder, encrypted and locked with a PIN
• Deleted items wait 30 days in Recently deleted, with Undo
• See details for any file, like size, dimensions and where a photo was taken
• Favourites, coloured tags, light and dark themes

Files has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything it keeps — your
settings, favourites, tags and Safe folder — stays on your phone.
