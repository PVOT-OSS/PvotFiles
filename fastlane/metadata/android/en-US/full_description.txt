Files shows what is on your phone's storage and helps you keep it tidy.

• See how much space you use and how much goes to photos, videos, audio and other files
• Browse every folder as a list or a grid, sorted by name, date, size or type
• Open files with your other apps, or share them
• Create folders, rename, copy and move — big copies keep going in the background
• Deleted items wait 30 days in Recently deleted, so you can restore them
• Find files by name, mark favourite folders and group files with your own tags
• Previews for photos and videos

Files has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything it keeps — your
view settings, favourites and tags — stays on your phone.
